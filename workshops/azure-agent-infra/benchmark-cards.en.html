7 / 7 · MY TAKE

Tiered multi-runtime orchestration strategy fits enterprise agent governance and cost control.

✅ Good fit
  • Coding Agent sandbox: deterministic graph lanes with explicit tool-approval checkpoints
  • Browser/CUA cluster: managed threads for lower orchestration ops overhead
  • Multi-tenant SaaS gateway: framework-level policy gates + tenant attribution
  • Financial zero-trust: strict HITL custody and immutable action lineage controls
⛔ Not for
  • Runtime chosen by convenience without control-flow determinism requirements
  • No HITL approval SLO and no timeout fallback policy
  • Shared governance policy across tenants without isolation dimensions
  • Self-hosted orchestration without operational ownership and rollback contracts
