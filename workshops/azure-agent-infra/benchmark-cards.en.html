7 / 7 · MY TAKE

Best for shared multi-tenant specialization fleets that need adapter-level governance and FinOps clarity.

✅ Good fit
  • 50+ tenant or vertical adapters with repeated specialization requests
  • Adapter signing, tenant isolation, and rollback policy are enforceable
  • Distilled SLM adapters with measurable chargeback and ROI gates
  • GPU pools shared across tenants with controlled hot-swap lifecycle
⛔ Not for
  • Single-tenant deployments with minimal adapter reuse
  • No adapter provenance/signing governance capabilities
  • Workloads requiring dedicated per-tenant full-model residency
  • Teams without operational ownership for adapter lifecycle controls
