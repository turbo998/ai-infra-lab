7 / 7 · MY TAKE

Bridge is often the practical default; silo is the compliance extreme; pool is the scale-cost baseline.

✅ Good fit
  • B2B SaaS platforms with tiered tenant isolation needs
  • Teams needing auditable anti-bleed controls and CMK workflows
  • Products balancing free/pro economics with enterprise isolation
  • Organizations enforcing quota fairness under bursty tenants
⛔ Not for
  • Single-tenant systems with no noisy-neighbor risk
  • Teams without key-management lifecycle ownership
  • Deployments unwilling to operate policy and audit controls
  • Scenarios where strict compliance isolation is unnecessary
