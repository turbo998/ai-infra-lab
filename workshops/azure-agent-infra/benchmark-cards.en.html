7 / 7 · MY TAKE

Dual-path realtime voice multimodal architecture fits enterprise voice agents balancing low latency and compliance.

✅ Good fit
  • Voice-first coding copilot with sub-300ms barge-in and deterministic turn-taking
  • Contact center agents requiring ACS Call Automation and SIP trunk interop
  • Multi-tenant SaaS voice bots with compliance recording and policy segmentation
  • Financial voice workflows needing auditable call custody and tool-call approval gates
⛔ Not for
  • No interruption handling strategy for user barge-in during model response
  • Tool-call orchestration without explicit turn-taking lock contracts
  • Compliance recording enabled without tenant retention and legal hold policy
  • Single-path voice runtime without fallback when regional media path degrades
