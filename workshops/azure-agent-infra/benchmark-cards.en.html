7 / 7 · MY TAKE

Durable Functions + Cosmos DB checkpoint stratification fits enterprise multi-agent recovery governance.

✅ Good fit
  • Coding Agent sandbox: resumable multi-step plans with deterministic checkpoint custody
  • Browser/CUA clusters: session-level replay and compensation step handoff
  • Multi-tenant SaaS gateway: tenant-isolated checkpoint partitions and replay quotas
  • Financial zero-trust: immutable checkpoint lineage with approval re-entry gates
⛔ Not for
  • No separation between workflow state and ephemeral conversational cache
  • Replay enabled without idempotency keys and compensation contracts
  • Cross-tenant checkpoint reuse or shared partition strategy
  • Checkpoint retention without lifecycle policy and legal audit mapping
