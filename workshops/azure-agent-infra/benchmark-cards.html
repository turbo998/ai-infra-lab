7 / 7 · MY TAKE

ERP Agent 想上线，必须把身份透传、干跑预检、连接池保护作为不可协商的发布门槛。

✅ Good fit
  • Large enterprises integrating SAP and mainframe systems
  • Teams with strict user-level authorization and audit requirements
  • Programs requiring precommit simulation before mutation
  • Operations enforcing connection-pool SLO protections
⛔ Not for
  • Workloads relying on shared privileged service accounts
  • No-governance direct mutation of core records
  • Teams without integration throttling and fallback discipline
  • Scenarios expecting RPA to replace API-first architecture
