7 / 7 · 我的结论

只要 OT 安全闸门由策略托管且不可绕过，即具备生产适配性。

✅ Good fit
  • factory cell operations: Hybrid OPC UA/Modbus ingest with twin context and deterministic safety gates.
  • predictive maintenance: Sensor trend scoring + CMMS ticket queue with cooldown and dedupe controls.
  • multi-site manufacturing governance: Recipe drift guardrails and cross-site policy replay lineage.
  • regulated OT safety environment: Strict read/write separation and immutable audit trails for every gated action.
⛔ Not for
  • factory cell operations: Direct LLM-triggered writes into PLC logic without owner-controlled approvals.
  • predictive maintenance: Alert-only workflows with no maintenance scheduling feedback loop.
  • multi-site manufacturing governance: Cross-site copy of production recipes without integrity checks.
  • regulated OT safety environment: Opaque automation paths without deterministic policy evidence.
