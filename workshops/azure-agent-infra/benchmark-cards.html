7 / 7 · MY TAKE

✅ Good fit
  • High-concurrency contact centers with mixed IVR/human workflows
  • Teams needing real-time whisper guidance for agents
  • Ops with strict PCI payment isolation controls
  • Programs optimizing containment and AHT together
⛔ Not for
  • No SIP/PSTN observability and line-quality governance
  • Single voice path without policy routing fallback
  • No media isolation for payment interactions
  • No warm-transfer context lifecycle controls
