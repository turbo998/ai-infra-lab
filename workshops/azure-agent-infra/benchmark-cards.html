7 / 7 · 我的结论

Azure 多模态摄取架构适合高复杂企业内容驱动的 Agent 检索与决策链路。

✅ Good fit
  • High-volume multimodal ingestion with partitioned fan-out and replay safety
  • Complex table/layout extraction combined with diagram and keyframe indexing
  • Unified vector retrieval over document, diagram, and video transcript chunks
  • Deterministic throughput and cost-per-10k-pages governance for enterprise SLAs
⛔ Not for
  • Single-thread ingestion with no partitioned backpressure handling
  • Ignoring nested-table structure quality before downstream retrieval indexing
  • No keyframe-transcript alignment strategy for video grounding
  • Missing cost controls and quota-aware batching for ingestion peaks
