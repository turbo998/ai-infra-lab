7 / 7 · MY TAKE

地理 Agent 要可落地，必须让“空间计算”和“路径求解”都可确定性复现。

✅ Good fit
  • Catastrophe insurance and corridor inspection pipelines
  • Supply-chain operations requiring weather-aware rerouting
  • Teams with geospatial data governance discipline
  • Platforms integrating EO imagery with transactional routing
⛔ Not for
  • LLM-only geometry reasoning without deterministic predicates
  • No CRS/index governance for geospatial data quality
  • No tile-window strategy and uncontrolled raster egress
  • No deterministic solver in logistics dispatch loop
