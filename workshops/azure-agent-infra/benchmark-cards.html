7 / 7 · 我的结论

当 AC-OPF 与 CIP 闸门保持强制时，具备强生产适配性。

✅ Good fit
  • VPP peak shaving and DER dispatch: Cloud advisory with AC-OPF gate and OpenADR fan-out control.
  • renewable ramp and curtailment mitigation: Weather ensemble forecast linked with deterministic line and transformer limits.
  • market bidding and balancing: Bid strategy constrained by dispatch feasibility and compliance boundaries.
  • outage crew and restoration planning: Telemetry-driven prioritization and policy-gated field dispatch actions.
⛔ Not for
  • VPP peak shaving and DER dispatch: Unconstrained LLM dispatch recommendations without physics verification.
  • renewable ramp and curtailment mitigation: Forecast-only routing without Kirchhoff/thermal checks.
  • market bidding and balancing: Aggressive bids that ignore feasible AC power-flow bounds.
  • outage crew and restoration planning: Fully autonomous control actions crossing BES boundaries.
