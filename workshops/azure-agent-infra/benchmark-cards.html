7 / 7 · 我的结论

自治 SRE 生产故障响应架构适合在高压告警场景下降低 MTTR 并约束修复风险。

✅ Good fit
  • Production platforms requiring autonomous triage with bounded remediation blast radius
  • Alert-storm deduplication pipelines tied to incident-priority orchestration
  • JIT approval gates for mutating ARM/kubectl actions under policy controls
  • MTTR benchmarking with read-only and mutating-path separation
⛔ Not for
  • Directly allowing mutating remediation without read-only triage evidence
  • No approval gate for high-impact ARM or kubectl changes
  • Alert storms handled with naive one-alert-one-runbook triggering
  • No side-effect containment for autonomous remediation loops
