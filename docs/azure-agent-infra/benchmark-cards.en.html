7 / 7 · MY TAKE

Legal agents are deployable only when full-document rewrite is forbidden and citation traceability is guaranteed.

✅ Good fit
  • Large M&A or procurement diligence workflows
  • Teams requiring clause-level redline auditability
  • Organizations enforcing privilege and ethical-wall controls
  • Programs demanding exact citation grounding
⛔ Not for
  • Full-document rewrite workflows without AST integrity checks
  • No matter-level privilege or index isolation controls
  • No legal sign-off chain for high-risk clause changes
  • No requirement for paragraph/span citation traceability
